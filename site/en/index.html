FAQ

Do I need to understand code?

No. You need to describe how your business works today. I build, run and monitor.

Where is my clients' data stored?

On a managed server for you, or on your own. I do not sell or share your data.

What if a workflow breaks?

I monitor everything and alert you immediately. A failure does not reach your client without my knowing — that is part of why clients stay.

Can I cancel?

Yes, monthly. But systems that solve a daily problem are among the last things a client cancels. I will tell you plainly if that is not your situation.
